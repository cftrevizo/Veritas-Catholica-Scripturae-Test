Catholic Study Topics

Learn the Faith · Ground it in Scripture · Read the Catechism · Defend it with charity

Sacraments are presented first, followed by core doctrine and formation. “Defending the Faith” then turns knowledge into disciplined dialogue with Protestant, Islamic, and historical objections.

Source boundaries: Faith Notebook citations preserve collected research; CCC references anchor official Catholic teaching; VCS explores cross-reference data; attributed Padre Luis Toro materials link to the specific public source. Debate scenarios are VCS formation aids, not Magisterial texts.

Dialogue standard: VCS aims to steelman the other person’s claim, define terms, identify burden of proof, test claims against primary sources, and close with an invitation to investigate rather than a victory declaration. 1 Peter 3:15–16 governs the tone: reasoned defense with gentleness and reverence.
