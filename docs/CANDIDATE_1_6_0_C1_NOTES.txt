VCS Website Edition 1.6.0 Candidate 1 — Source Library Infrastructure

Adds:
- source-library.html searchable interface
- data/source_library.json and .csv
- 33 normalized source records spanning Apostolic Fathers, major patristic witnesses, CCC, CCEL and Fordham discovery layers
- provenance/licensing notes and topic tags
- navigation links from core VCS pages

Methodological rule: this index is a discovery/provenance layer. Topic tags do not by themselves prove doctrinal continuity. Historical translations are not silently treated as modern critical editions.
