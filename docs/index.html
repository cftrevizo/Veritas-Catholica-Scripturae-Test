Method

What the levels mean

Level A

Supported by at least two independent established source families.

Level B

Supported by one established non-interpretive source family and published with attribution.

Scholar Review

Interpretive/intertextual relationships retained separately rather than presented as settled conclusions.

Important: A cross-reference does not by itself establish direct quotation, literary dependence, typology, inspiration, or Catholic doctrine. It identifies a relationship worth studying. See the repository methodology and source documentation for provenance and limitations.
