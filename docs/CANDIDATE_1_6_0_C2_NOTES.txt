VCS Website Edition 1.6.0 Candidate 2 — Semantic Historical Timeline

Adds Timeline as the fifth Visual Experience mode.
- Contextual controls: Timeline hides unrelated graph controls; its filters change with scope.
- Scopes: All History, Old Testament, NT & Apostolic Age, Church History.
- Five detail levels: Foundational, Major, Significant, Detailed, Source-level.
- OT categories include biblical events, patriarchs, covenants, kings, prophets, empires/world context, messianic, Scripture/canon.
- Church categories include Fathers, councils, doctrine, schisms, reforms, persecution/martyrs, Scripture/canon, missions/saints, world context.
- Schisms and reforms are distinct categories.
- Dates carry quality labels where chronology is approximate/traditional.
- Timeline events are a curated initial layer, not an exhaustive chronology.

Candidate 1 Source Library remains included. Production v1.5.0 should remain untouched while testing.
