Methodology

How Edition 1 was reconciled

  1. Reuse before invention. Established datasets do the heavy lifting.
  2. Provenance first. Supporting source families remain identifiable.
  3. Catholic scope. The research model includes the 73-book Catholic canon.
  4. No silent theological inference. A cross-reference is not automatically labeled quotation, allusion, typology, or doctrine.
  5. Scholar review is non-blocking. Interpretive items are isolated instead of delaying source-supported publication.

The general backbone was reconstructed from raw OpenBible and SoulLiberty/TSK-derived files included in the NEUU source package and reconciled with the cross-reference files shipped in The Catholic Bible. Duplicate normalized directed endpoint pairs were merged while source flags were retained.

Verification

What the levels mean

Level A

Two or more independent established source families support the normalized relationship.

Level B

One established source family supports the relationship with provenance.

Scholar Review

Authored or interpretive material is retained separately for scholarly adjudication.

Interpretation boundary: a cross-reference does not by itself establish direct quotation, literary dependence, typology, inspiration, or Catholic doctrine.

Known limitations

What still deserves review

  • Edition 1 contains 673 relationships intentionally held for scholarly review.
  • The seven Deuterocanonical books have 920 relationships in Edition 1, including 701 Deuterocanon ↔ New Testament relationships.
  • Catholic Esther and Daniel require special versification care; VCS avoids silently forcing incompatible numbering systems.
  • The Catholic Bible documentation reports 207,636 cross-references, while the 73 shipped JSON files inspected for Edition 1 contained 213,267 records. VCS records the shipped data and discloses the discrepancy.

Peer review & corrections

Help improve the concordance

Researchers and Catholic biblical scholars are invited to report mapping errors, challenge classifications, supply textual evidence, or propose corrections through GitHub Issues. Review should identify the VCS reference(s), the disputed claim or mapping, supporting primary/secondary sources, and the proposed disposition.
